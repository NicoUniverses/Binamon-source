The story

From Buenos Aires to the BNB Chain

  • Jun 2021

    Binamon is born

    A crypto-gaming company sets out to build an AAA+ game on Binance Smart Chain. The $BMON token powers the economy: every Binamon NFT is minted by burning $BMON through the Booster contract, so supply shrinks as the collection grows.

  • Jul 2021

    Genesis NFTs & staking

    Booster packs reveal three Binamons each, with ultra-rare variants. Holders can battle, trade or stake their monsters for passive rewards, with farms on PancakeSwap, Alpaca Finance and BakerySwap.

  • 23 Aug 2021

    All-time high

    $BMON reaches $0.88 with a record $82 million traded in a single day. Listings follow on KuCoin, Gate.io, LBank and a dozen more exchanges.

  • Aug – Oct 2021

    Binamon Runner

    The first play-to-earn game of the metaverse, for PC and Android. Players locked a Z1 NFT to run, fight with elemental combos and earn BMON-Z1 tokens — a record of more than 80,000 human hours played per day.

  • 1 Oct 2021

    MOBA Arena, first gameplay

    The AAA multiplayer arena built in Unreal Engine shows its first development gameplay. Production later involved Starloop Studios.

  • Nov 2021

    “King of the dApps”

    BSC News crowns Binamon King of the dApps (13 Nov 2021) and it is nominated Most Valuable Builder of the BSC. Binance NFT launches “Binamon Forest Lands” on its marketplace.

  • 2022

    From play-to-earn to win-to-earn

    Runner is retired and replaced by a more sustainable model. Binamon Firewars, a real-time multiplayer shooter, pays only those who win: each match costs a 5 BMON ticket, split between players, the reward pool and the project. FUD Collection and Lands join the map.

  • 25 Jan 2023

    The DAO

    The founding team steps down and hands the project to a community DAO. Ownership of the BMON contract is renounced on-chain. Binamon becomes a legacy.

Binamon DAO

The project now belongs to the community

On 25 January 2023 the founding team announced that Binamon would pass to a DAO, to be created, maintained and administered by the community for the continuity of the project. The former centralized channels stopped being official, and ownership of the BMON contract was renounced on-chain.

“Our greatest pride would be to see that Binamon’s legacy continues.”

Every token used to mint a Binamon NFT was burned by public, open-source contracts. The remaining treasury and the source code are reserved for the DAO.
